Privacy

Every live chat, one bubble.

Bubblecast is a desktop app for streamers. It brings Twitch, YouTube, TikTok and Kick live chat together in one OBS dock and a chat overlay for your stream.

One dock

Read and reply to every platform's chat from a single panel inside OBS.

Stream overlay

Show chat, Super Chats, memberships and channel point redemptions on stream in comic-style bubbles.

Polls everywhere

Start one poll on Twitch and YouTube at once and see the combined results.

Runs on your PC

No Bubblecast servers. Your sign-ins and chat stay on your own computer.

Official connections

Bubblecast connects to Twitch and YouTube through their official APIs. When you connect YouTube, Google asks you to allow Bubblecast to manage your YouTube account. Bubblecast uses that permission only to read your live chat, send the messages you write, run the polls you start and show your live viewer count. Read the privacy policy for details.
